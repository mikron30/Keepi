App name
Keepi

Short description
Scan, organize and get more value from the things you already own

Full description
Keepi helps you know what you own, where it is, and what you can do with it.

Take a photo or choose one from your library and Keepi uses AI to identify the item, suggest a category, description and estimated value, then save it to your personal inventory.

With Keepi you can:
• Manage a personal inventory of almost anything
• Scan one item or multiple items from a single photo
• Save photos, descriptions, condition, estimated value and expiry dates
• Keep items private or offer them for sale, rent, lending, exchange or giveaway
• Discover relevant nearby items using a location you choose to save
• Message other users
• Report content or users and block users
• Manage privacy and request account/data deletion

Keepi is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

AI-generated recognition, prices, condition estimates and expiry-date readings are estimates only and should be verified before you rely on them.
