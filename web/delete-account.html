Delete your Keepi account

You can request deletion of your Keepi account and associated personal data from this page.

How to request deletion

  1. Use the email address associated with your Keepi account.
  2. Send an email to mikron30@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Keepi account.
  3. Include the email address used for your Keepi account so we can verify the request.

Request account deletion

What will be deleted

After verification, the request covers the Keepi account and personal data associated with it, including profile information, private inventory data, uploaded item photos and account-linked user content, subject to data that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations or dispute resolution.

What happens next

We may contact you to verify account ownership before deleting data. Once the deletion request is completed, access to the Keepi account and deleted data cannot be restored.

Questions? Contact mikron30@gmail.com.
