Keepi Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Keepi helps people organize items they own and, when they choose, make items available to other users. This policy explains what information Keepi collects, why it is used, and the choices available to users.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, profile photo, sign-in provider and, if you add it, a phone number.
  • Location: precise home or current location only when you choose to save it or use nearby features.
  • Photos and item information: photos you take or select, item names, categories, descriptions, values, expiry dates, marketplace options and related inventory data.
  • Messages and user-generated content: messages sent through Keepi and information you publish or share with other users.
  • Technical and advertising data: device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions and diagnostic information may be processed by Google Mobile Ads and related Google services.

How we use information

  • Provide account sign-in and Keepi features.
  • Store and synchronize your inventory and profile.
  • Provide nearby discovery and marketplace functionality when requested.
  • Analyze item photos with Google/Firebase AI features to identify and describe items.
  • Deliver and measure advertising through Google AdMob.
  • Protect the service, diagnose problems, prevent abuse and improve Keepi.

Service providers and sharing

Keepi uses Google services including Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Firebase Storage, Firebase/Google Cloud AI services and Google AdMob. Information may be processed by these providers as necessary to provide the service. Information you deliberately publish or send to another user may be visible to those users according to the feature you use.

Location and photos

Keepi requests camera, photo-library or location access only when a feature needs it. You can deny or revoke device permissions in your device settings. Exact home/current coordinates are stored as part of your private profile when you choose to save them.

Advertising

Keepi may display ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may process identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, interactions and diagnostic data for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and related purposes, subject to Google policies and applicable consent requirements.

Data retention and deletion

We keep information while your Keepi account is active and as needed to provide the service. You can request deletion of your Keepi account and associated personal data at any time. Some limited information may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance or dispute resolution.

To request deletion, visit Delete your Keepi account.

Security

Keepi uses Firebase and Google Cloud security controls and access rules to protect stored data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Keepi is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18.

Your choices

You can choose whether to provide optional phone, location and item information, control app permissions on your device, sign out, and request deletion of your account and data.
